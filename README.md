I'm a Digital Game Design student at Halic University in Istanbul who likes building things from the ground up. Games are the main thread, mostly Unity and Unreal, but a lot of my time goes into full applications outside games too: a terminal emulator, a local voice assistant, an operations platform. When a tool doesn't work the way I want it to, I usually end up writing my own.

Right now I'm learning Rust, digging into shaders, and finishing my final year. Open to studio internships, indie collabs and freelance work.
